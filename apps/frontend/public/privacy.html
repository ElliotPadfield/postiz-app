Privacy and data use

Effective 2 November 2026

Elliot Content is an internal tool operated by Elliot Padfield for managing authorized Futurecraft and personal social accounts.

Information accessed and stored

With the account owner’s authorization, the tool accesses account and channel identity, profile images, published content identifiers and captions, and available performance reports such as views, interactions, watch time and retention. It stores connection credentials, drafts, uploaded media, scheduling records, published-post references and analytics snapshots on its application server.

How information is used

Account data is used to identify the connected profile, plan and publish content selected by an authorized operator, import existing posts and display their performance. YouTube data is obtained through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. The tool does not sell account data, use it for advertising, or build advertising audiences.

The tool’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google account data is not used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models by this tool.

Access and sharing

Workspace information is accessible to authorized operators. Content and account credentials are sent to a connected platform as needed to carry out the requested operation. The server and its hosting infrastructure process the records needed to run the tool. The tool does not provide public access to connected-account credentials or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Removing a post, media item or account in the interface may hide it or mark it as deleted while its database record, credentials, files or backups remain. Permanent removal requires administrator action. Contact the administrator to request deletion of stored records, credentials, media and applicable backups. Records remain stored until that deletion is carried out.

A local account disconnect does not necessarily revoke credentials at the platform. Revoke the tool's authorization directly at the platform to stop further API access using that authorization.

You can revoke the tool’s Google access through Google account permissions. Revocation does not delete posts already published on a platform. Use that platform’s own controls to manage published content.

Contact

Contact the operator, Elliot Padfield, through the support address shown on this tool’s Google authorization screen or through the administrator who provided your access.
