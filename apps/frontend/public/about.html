Elliot Content

An internal content workspace operated by Elliot Padfield for Futurecraft and personal social profiles.

The tool plans content, stores media and drafts, schedules approved posts, and collects analytics from connected accounts. Futurecraft videos can be planned across selected profiles together; personal posts can be planned separately for each profile.

Google authorization connects the operator’s own YouTube channels. It enables channel selection, reading existing public uploads and their performance, and uploading content when an authorized operator schedules it.

Access is restricted to authorized operators. Public registration is disabled. Connecting an account does not itself publish content.

Read the privacy and data-use policy and conditions for internal use before connecting an account.
